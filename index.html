escalation time is what a person spends on a task the agent tried and handed back. it is often longer than today's handle time, because the easy ones are gone.

pricing model

token pricing is charged on every run, including the ones that end in escalation.

per-resolution pricing is charged only on tasks the agent finishes.

justify: saves money and pays back in 6 months or less. marginal: saves money, payback 6 to 18 months. kill: loses money every month, or payback longer than 18 months.

sensitivity: net monthly savings

rows move the automation rate; columns multiply the agent cost (usage plus platform fee). your current case is shaded. a minus sign means the agent costs more than it saves.

how it's calculated

human today = tasks × handle min ÷ 60 × cost per hour

human after = tasks × (1 − automation) × escalation min ÷ 60 × cost per hour

agent, token-based = tasks × (tokens ÷ 1,000,000 × price per million + overhead) + platform fee

agent, per resolution = tasks × automation × price per resolution + platform fee

net monthly = human today − human after − agent

payback months = implementation ÷ net monthly (only when net is above zero)

first-year net = net monthly × 12 − implementation

what this leaves out: ramp-up months, quality cost of wrong answers, headcount you cannot actually release, and change management. if the verdict only holds at the top row of the sensitivity table, treat it as a kill.
